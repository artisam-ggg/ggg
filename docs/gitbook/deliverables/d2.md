# D2 — Single-sig, N-winner, and TTL

## SOW commitment

Perform deploy-and-initialize in one transaction, support 1–10 winners, validate storage TTL behavior, expose `get_players()` and `get_tournament()` helpers, and retain at least 30 contract tests.

## Implemented design

The constructor receives the organizer, referee, token/asset configuration, entry fee, winner distribution, and settlement deadline in the same deployment operation. The contract's `finalize_results` path supports one through ten winners. Distribution basis points must be valid and sum to 10,000; rounding dust is deterministically assigned to rank one.

The read helpers expose registration-ordered players and a tournament snapshot containing immutable configuration, status, winner order, player count, and settlement deadline. The TTL policy extends instance and code storage on state-changing paths when required, while read helpers are designed not to extend TTL merely by reading state.

## Known proof boundary

The existing evidence records a Testnet upload of the escrow WASM. To satisfy the full deliverable acceptance criteria, the final report still needs proof of a deploy-and-initialize transaction, a contract ID, and a live N-winner distribution on that instance.

## Reproducible payout example

The #219 five-winner test starts with five registrations at `1,000,003` base units each: a pool of `5,000,015`. Its 40% / 25% / 15% / 10% / 10% split pays `2,000,008`, `1,250,003`, `750,002`, `500,001`, and `500,001` base units. Two units of floor-rounding dust go to rank one, the total payout equals the pool, and the test checks a zero escrow balance.
